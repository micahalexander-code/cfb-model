Confidence

How much we trust each team to cover our teased line, out of 100. Team (50): the team's record against our teased line in every game it's played. When we picked them it's our teaser result; when we picked their opponent it's the reverse. Undefeated = 50, .500 = 25, winless = 0. A team needs 5 games against our teased line before it gets a score. Edge (50): how often picks in the edge range of the team's matchup this week have covered the teased line (History tab, teaser column). On Sunday and Monday that's the coming week. Teams that already played this week, are on a bye, or don't have a line yet use their most recent game. 100% = 50. No data yet counts as a coin flip (25).

Line Tracking

How each game's DraftKings spread and our edge move through the week. The time axis runs from Sunday (when next week's lines post, right after Saturday's games) to kickoff (dashed line). Three times a day (9 am, 3 pm and 10 pm Eastern) the model saves one point per game: the line and the edge as they stood then, so the first dot is Sunday at the earliest. The last dot is the live line right now, or the line at kickoff once the game starts. Edge moves when the line moves or Sagarin updates his ratings. The DK line is the home team's spread (minus = home favored) on the left axis; edge is on the right axis.
