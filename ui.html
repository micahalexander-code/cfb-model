Confidence

How much we trust each team to cover a 3-point teaser, out of 100. Two halves, 50 points each:
Team (50): the team's record against its own DraftKings closing spread teased 3 points, in every game it has played this season (any pick type, all weeks). 100% = 50, .500 = 25. A team needs 5 graded games to get a score.
Edge (50): how often our Core Model picks in the edge range of the team's game this week have covered the teased line (table at the bottom). On Sunday and Monday that's the coming week. A team that already played this week or is on a bye uses its most recent game.
The bar shows the two halves: team, edge.

Parlays

Our teaser picks (The Core Model only, each teased 3) paired into 2-leg parlays. A leg qualifies when the team we picked has a confidence score at or above the minimum going into that week (Tuesday), so past parlays use only what was known before the week started. Kickoff times play no part: each week (Tue–Sat) the two highest-confidence legs are paired, then the next two, and so on. An odd leg left over isn't used. A parlay wins only if every leg covers its teased line (a pushed leg drops out). DraftKings alt-line prices aren't in our data, so odds and units are estimates from the assumed price per leg.

Line Tracking

How each game's DraftKings spread and our edge move through the week. The time axis runs from Sunday (when next week's lines post, right after Saturday's games) to kickoff (dashed line). Three times a day (9 am, 3 pm and 10 pm Eastern) the model saves one point per game: the line and the edge as they stood then, so the first dot is Sunday at the earliest. The last dot is the live line right now, or the line at kickoff once the game starts. Edge moves when the line moves or Sagarin updates his ratings. The DK line is the home team's spread (minus = home favored) on the left axis; edge is on the right axis.
